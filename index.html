Qameti System

Run a savings committee (qameti / ROSCA) without paperwork. Add your members, let the system draw numbers and allot each member a payout month automatically, record the monthly entries, and get a clean report for every month.

Step 1Create a qameti and set the monthly amount.
Step 2Add all members who will take part.
Step 3Draw numbers — months are allotted automatically.
Step 4Mark monthly entries and generate the report.
